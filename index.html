What we wrap

Whole cars, one hood, or the trim around your headlights. Vinyl goes over the factory paint and protects it, and it can come off later if you change your mind.

Ask about your vehicle

  1. Full color change

    Gloss, satin, matte or metallic, in whatever color you can find a swatch for. No paint booth and no repaint.

  2. Commercial and trailer wraps

    Logo, services and phone number on trucks, cars and enclosed trailers. Bring your logo and we'll talk through the layout.

Questions people ask

How much does a wrap cost?

It depends on the vehicle, how much of it gets wrapped, and the film. A trim piece or a hood costs far less than a full color change. Quotes are free, so call, text or send the form above.

Can I pick an exact color?

Yes. We sit down with you and go through colors and finishes (gloss, matte, satin, metallic, carbon fiber) until you find the one you want.

Will a wrap hurt my paint?

Vinyl goes on over the factory paint and covers it. When it comes off, the paint under it has been protected the whole time.

Do you wrap trailers and work trucks?

Yes. High Plains Custom Metal and Sunflower Designs both have trailer wraps from our shop. Bring your logo and we'll work out the layout with you.

Do you do small jobs?

All the time. Radio bezels, badges, window trim, a roof or a hood. Some of our favorite reviews are from small jobs.
